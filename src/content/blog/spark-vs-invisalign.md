## FAQ

### What are the primary differences between Spark and Invisalign material?

Spark aligners are made from TruGEN material. It's clear and doesn't stain easily. Invisalign uses SmartTrack material for gentle, steady force. Both are comfy, but Spark has special edges to avoid gum irritation.

### How does treatment time vary between these two clear aligner systems?

Treatment time depends on how complex your case is and how well you wear the aligners. Both Spark and Invisalign take about 6 to 18 months. Spark might move teeth a bit faster, but wearing them for 22 hours a day is key.
