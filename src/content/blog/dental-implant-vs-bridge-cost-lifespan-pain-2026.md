### Comparing Lifespan and Durability

Implants and bridges are very different. An implant is like a new root, fitting into your jaw. Bridges use your teeth for support, which can stress them.

> "The longevity of a dental restoration is directly tied to the quality of the foundation and the patient's commitment to daily oral hygiene."

Implants can last 20 years or more with good care. Bridges need to be replaced every 10 to 15 years as they wear down.

### Managing Pain and Recovery Expectations

People worry about implant surgery more than bridges. But, most find the pain from implants manageable. Over-the-counter meds usually help with the soreness after surgery.

*   **Implants:** Need surgery and healing time for bone integration.
*   **Bridges:** Don't need surgery, just prepare existing teeth for crowns.
*   **Recovery:** Most people can go back to normal in a few days after either procedure.

Your dentist will help with a recovery plan. Knowing these facts helps you feel more confident and calm about your treatment.

### Dental Bridges: Pros and Cons

Dental bridges are a quick fix for missing teeth. They attach a fake tooth to the real ones next to the gap.

*   **Pros:** They are fast and need fewer dentist visits. They are also cheaper than other options at first.
*   **Cons:** They don't replace the tooth root, which can cause bone loss. They might also hurt the healthy teeth next to the gap.

Think about using a [dental implant pricing tool](/cost) to compare costs. Bridges might seem cheaper, but you might need to replace them later. See our [implant FAQs](/faq) for how insurance typically handles each option.

### Dental Implants: Pros and Cons

Dental implants are a better choice for lasting results. They replace the whole tooth, including the root. A good tooth implant cost calculator shows the cost of this top-notch fix.

*   **Pros:** Implants feel and look like real teeth. They don't harm the teeth next to them. They can last for many years with care.
*   **Cons:** Getting an implant takes longer and needs surgery. It costs more than a bridge at first.
